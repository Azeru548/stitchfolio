FAQ
Do you do hourly work?

No — fixed scopes and retainers only. Hourly penalizes efficiency. You get a written scope, fixed price and dates before we start.

Who owns the code?

You do, on final payment. MIT-licensed starter included where relevant; secrets and keys never leave your vault.

Payment terms?

50% kickoff, 30% beta, 20% handover. Engineering engagements are invoiced in USD by wire or USDC. Starter builds are invoiced in naira. Bank transfer is available for naira invoices.

How fast can we start?

Usually 1–2 weeks out. Rescue sprints can start faster if the repo and credentials are ready.

Do you maintain after launch?

Yes — the support window in the scope is included. After that, a monthly retainer covers fixes, deps, small features and monitoring.

NDA / contracts?

Happy to sign your NDA. Scope, price, dates and IP go in writing before kickoff.
